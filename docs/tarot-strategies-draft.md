# Tarot strategies — draft (one per Rider-Waite-Smith card)

Status: in review, batch by batch. Approved batches are final unless the developer reopens them.
Order: Major Arcana (22), Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles (14 each).

## Batch 1 — Major Arcana (approved 2026-10-17)

| # | Card | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | The Fool | Be the beginner on purpose. |
| 1 | The Magician | Change the order and see what breaks. |
| 2 | The High Priestess | Work on the part nobody will check. |
| 3 | The Empress | Say it to the wall first. |
| 4 | The Emperor | Make a rule and break it halfway through. |
| 5 | The Hierophant | Ask the dumbest question in the room. |
| 6 | The Lovers | Take away the thing you lean on. |
| 7 | The Chariot | Finish the sentence differently. |
| 8 | Strength | Give it a deadline that's too soon. |
| 9 | The Hermit | Do the opposite of your last decision. |
| 10 | Wheel of Fortune | Copy something badly on purpose. |
| 11 | Justice | Spend longer on the part that bores you. |
| 12 | The Hanged Man | Remove the explanation. |
| 13 | Death | Use the old ending as the new beginning. |
| 14 | Temperance | Work as if no one will ever see it. |
| 15 | The Devil | Trade the best part for a worse part that's yours. |
| 16 | The Tower | Put it down for a day and pick up the thing next to it. |
| 17 | The Star | Pretend it's already been done, and do it anyway. |
| 18 | The Moon | Argue for the idea you dislike. |
| 19 | The Sun | Make the small thing enormous. |
| 20 | Judgement | Let someone else's mistake be yours. |
| 21 | The World | Stop explaining where this is going. |

## Batch 2 — Wands (approved 2026-10-17)

| Card | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Ace of Wands | Start with the last line. |
| Two of Wands | Write down what you're sure of, then cross out half. |
| Three of Wands | Show it to the person least likely to get it. |
| Four of Wands | Let the rough edge be the finished edge. |
| Five of Wands | Make two versions fight, and keep the loser. |
| Six of Wands | Skip the part you're proud of. |
| Seven of Wands | Defend the weakest idea for ten minutes. |
| Eight of Wands | Go faster than is comfortable and don't look back. |
| Nine of Wands | Take the half-finished one off the shelf. |
| Ten of Wands | Put down something you've been carrying out of habit. |
| Page of Wands | Try it with the wrong tool. |
| Knight of Wands | Say yes to the next thing that comes along. |
| Queen of Wands | Make it louder than you're allowed. |
| King of Wands | Give the job to the version of you who doesn't hesitate. |

## Batch 3 — Cups (accepted 2026-10-17, no changes requested)

| Card | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Ace of Cups | Say the thing you'd only say to a friend. |
| Two of Cups | Swap places with whoever disagrees with you. |
| Three of Cups | Let the others finish it. |
| Four of Cups | Ignore the obvious offer and look at what's beside it. |
| Five of Cups | Go back for what you threw out. |
| Six of Cups | Return to the first version you loved. |
| Seven of Cups | Choose by closing your eyes. |
| Eight of Cups | Walk away from the part that's working. |
| Nine of Cups | Keep the easy win and stop there. |
| Ten of Cups | Stop improving it. |
| Page of Cups | Believe the odd idea for one full minute. |
| Knight of Cups | Follow the feeling, not the plan. |
| Queen of Cups | Slow down until you can hear it. |
| King of Cups | Stay calm about the part that's going wrong. |

## Batch 4 — Swords (accepted 2026-10-17, no changes requested)

| Card | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Ace of Swords | Cut it to one sentence. |
| Two of Swords | Don't decide yet. |
| Three of Swords | Keep the sentence that hurts. |
| Four of Swords | Do nothing for exactly as long as it takes. |
| Five of Swords | Win the argument, then give the point away. |
| Six of Swords | Carry only what fits in one hand. |
| Seven of Swords | Borrow the method, not the result. |
| Eight of Swords | Notice which limit you made up. |
| Nine of Swords | Write down the worst version and read it out loud. |
| Ten of Swords | Let it be over. |
| Page of Swords | Check the claim nobody's checked. |
| Knight of Swords | Say the blunt version first. |
| Queen of Swords | Remove every word that's there to be liked. |
| King of Swords | Make the rule, then explain who it's for. |

## Batch 5 — Pentacles (approved 2026-10-17)

| Card | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Ace of Pentacles | Start with something you can hold. |
| Two of Pentacles | Do two things badly at once. |
| Three of Pentacles | Ask someone to fix the part you can't see. |
| Four of Pentacles | Let go of one thing you've been guarding. |
| Five of Pentacles | Use what's left over. |
| Six of Pentacles | Give away your best idea, unsigned. |
| Seven of Pentacles | Wait. Check it again tomorrow. |
| Eight of Pentacles | Repeat the dull step until it changes. |
| Nine of Pentacles | Do it for yourself, in private. |
| Ten of Pentacles | Make something that will outlast the reason you made it. |
| Page of Pentacles | Study one small thing too closely. |
| Knight of Pentacles | Take the slow route on purpose. |
| Queen of Pentacles | Make the useful thing comfortable. |
| King of Pentacles | Build it so it can be left alone. |
