# Oblique Strategies Deck

60 cards. Three tiers. Tier labels are for the deck design, not for display.

## Tier 1 — Working Instructions

1. Delete the first thing you wrote.
2. Do it again but worse.
3. Whatever you think it is, it's the other thing.
4. Stop before you're done.
5. Use only the words you already have.
6. The real version is hiding behind a sillier one.
7. Let the weakest part take over.
8. Describe it to someone who's never heard of any of it.
9. What if the stakes were smaller.
10. Find the part you don't want to cut, cut it.
11. The second-best idea is probably right.
12. Leave something unresolved and walk away.
13. Pick the option you haven't been thinking about.
14. Say the quiet part and stop talking.
15. You've been avoiding the obvious move on purpose.
16. Make it shorter until it's barely anything.
17. The constraint you hate is the one to keep.
18. Treat it like you've already finished it.
19. What would a bored version of you do here.
20. Replace one word with its opposite.
21. The mistake stays in.
22. Start over without looking at the old one.
23. If it feels clever it's probably wrong.
24. Do the boring version and see what happens.
25. Whatever you were going to do, don't.
26. Turn the most polished part into the least polished part.
27. The voice you're using isn't yours. Try the other one.
28. Make it embarrassing.
29. The audience isn't who you think it is.
30. Cut anything that sounds like it was written to sound good.

## Tier 2 — Oblique Prompts

31. You are allowed to be wrong on purpose.
32. A real poster posts even if there's no audience.
33. Ask what would disappoint you, go do that.
34. The point is hidden in the part you think is throat-clearing.
35. Listen to the thing that's been annoying you.
36. Nothing has to mean anything right now.
37. Keep the awkward part.
38. Your taste is the obstacle.
39. Pretend you don't know what this is supposed to be.
40. The interesting thing happened ten minutes ago.
41. Something you already know is the answer.
42. Say less. Like, way less.
43. Rest is a form of making.
44. There is no serious goose.
45. Take the setup and skip the punchline.
46. Add something that doesn't belong.
47. Whatever came out first, use that.
48. Follow the thread you almost abandoned.
49. The embarrassment is the information.
50. You can change your mind in public.

## Tier 3 — Rare

51. The material you were afraid of is now just where you stand. Use it.
52. Add the impossible element.
53. Reach for the weird backup thing you've been carrying.
54. Stop using the real name. Describe what it looks like.
55. Your grand romantic vision has practical problems.
56. Consider surrender.
57. Your problem isn't as complicated as you're making it.
58. Don't trust what you've been told about the finish.
59. Stay with almost-getting-it.
60. You're fishing for agreement. Notice that.
